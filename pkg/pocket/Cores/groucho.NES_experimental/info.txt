NES_experimental: experimental derivative of agg23's Pocket port and MiSTer's NES core.
Not an official agg23 release.
Core by Ludvig Strigeus, sorgelig, greyrogue, Kitrinx, paulb-nl, Adam Gastineau and other contributors.
Nine blend modes from snckrz's NES Analogizer fork, based on Kitrinx's filter.
